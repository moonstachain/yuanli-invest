# SDR0 Human Review Card v0.1

Status: `candidate_ready_for_human_review`

Human Gate:

`ACCEPT_SDR0_SOVEREIGN_DEBT_REPRESSION_REAL_VALUE_DILUTION_DESIGN`

## 1. Review question

Should `Sovereign Debt Repression & Real-Value Dilution` be accepted as a specialized `CAP-R-01` profile for diagnosing transitions from ordinary sovereign market financing toward persistent policy-induced funding-cost suppression and real creditor dilution?

## 2. Recommended judgment

**ACCEPT WITH HARD BOUNDARIES.**

The profile fills a real research gap, but only if it remains a typed regime-decomposition capability and does not become a macro narrative shortcut called “soft default.”

## 3. Seven acceptance tests

### A. Ontology

- [ ] Remains under `CAP-R-01`.
- [ ] Does not create a new top-level `Credit World`, `Debt Engine` or parallel investment constitution.

### B. Stable question

- [ ] Asks whether sovereign funding pressure is translating into repression / real-value dilution mechanisms.
- [ ] Does not ask for a binary prediction of default.

### C. Mechanism separation

- [ ] `liquidity_backstop != financial_repression`.
- [ ] `financial_repression != real_value_dilution`.
- [ ] `real_value_dilution != explicit_legal_default`.

### D. Hard gate

- [ ] `real_value_dilution_active` requires the full AND gate.
- [ ] Any “2-of-4” soft-default voting rule is prohibited.

### E. Evidence

- [ ] Primary policy/fiscal/auction/holdings evidence is required for live state.
- [ ] Historical analogies cannot prove 2026.
- [ ] Theory/source seeds are not silently promoted into Canon.

### F. Replay discipline

- [ ] U.S. 1942–1951 and Japan 2016–2024 are mandatory Gold Replays.
- [ ] U.S. 2020 QE, UK 2022 LDI and FIMA are mandatory hard negatives / boundary cases.
- [ ] 2026 U.S. × Japan is Point-in-Time Shadow, not retrospective storytelling.

### G. Authority

- [ ] No target price, recommended weight, buy/sell/hold, position size or live execution.
- [ ] Research pass does not imply capital pass.

## 4. Key architecture decision

The decisive choice is **profile vs new capability**.

QXM1 already froze the principle that credit/debt-cycle mechanics are an implementation profile of `CAP-R-01 | Regime Causal Decomposition` because the mother question remains the same: how the capital regime transmits through funding, discount rates, balance sheets and risk appetite.

SDR0 follows that precedent.

Creating a standalone `CAP-SDR-*` at this stage would duplicate macro-regime ontology before evidence proves the need.

## 5. Key epistemic decision

The phrase `soft default` is allowed only as an external narrative / search term. It is not a canonical ResearchState.

Canonical mechanism vocabulary is:

```text
fiscal_constraint
funding_market_stress
liquidity_backstop
financial_repression
real_rate_suppression
real_value_dilution
policy_normalization_or_exit
```

This avoids a common failure mode where one emotionally strong label silently collapses several distinct mechanisms.

## 6. Key causal decision

Japan is modeled as a possible **catalyst / marginal-demand transmission node**, not the cause of U.S. fiscal stress.

The 2026 Shadow must separately test:

```text
U.S. fiscal constraint
×
Treasury marginal-demand elasticity
×
Japan home-yield / FX-hedging / repatriation pressure
×
U.S. policy response
```

The shadow fails if “Japan sells Treasuries” is treated as a sufficient causal explanation.

## 7. Gold Replay purpose

### U.S. 1942–1951

Tests a high-confidence case where the Fed explicitly supported low sovereign financing rates and the regime eventually collided with inflation pressure.

### Japan 2016–2024

Tests a long yield-target regime with massive central-bank duration absorption and an observable exit path. It is intentionally useful because it can separate **yield suppression** from **real-value dilution** rather than assuming both are always present.

## 8. Hard-negative purpose

The hard negatives are as important as the Gold cases:

- U.S. 2020 QE tests whether the profile falsely labels emergency macro stabilization as fiscal dominance.
- UK 2022 LDI tests whether emergency sovereign-bond purchases for market functioning are falsely labeled repression.
- FIMA tests whether collateralized dollar liquidity that prevents Treasury sales is falsely labeled monetization.

If SDR0 cannot distinguish these, the capability should not be promoted.

## 9. Expected value if accepted

SDR0 would add a reusable macro capability for questions such as:

- Is a high-debt sovereign merely expensive to finance, or entering a repression regime?
- Is a central-bank purchase program ordinary monetary easing, market-function support, or fiscal funding support?
- Are negative real yields accidental macro outcomes or an economically persistent debt-liquidation mechanism?
- Is foreign reserve repatriation a catalyst, a transient flow, or a binding marginal-demand shock?
- When should gold / FX / inflation-linked / duration repricing be interpreted as a regime symptom rather than proof of the mechanism?

## 10. What acceptance does NOT mean

Acceptance does not mean:

- the United States is in soft default;
- 2026 is already a financial-repression regime;
- gold must rise;
- Treasuries must fall;
- Japan will sell Treasuries;
- the profile has passed benchmark or Replay;
- the profile has trading authority.

It means only that the **research contract is well-formed enough to be tested against history and current reality.**

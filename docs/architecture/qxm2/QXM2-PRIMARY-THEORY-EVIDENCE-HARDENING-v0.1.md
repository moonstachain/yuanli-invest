## Authority law

`Claim Authority <= Evidence Authority`.

QXM2 preserves five non-equivalences:

- Shadow != Registry.
- Primary Source Verified != Theory Admitted.
- Theory Supported != Hypothesis Supported.
- Hypothesis Supported != Capability Qualified.
- Benchmark Specified != Benchmark Passed.

Mechanism support also does not imply forecasting support.

Qin Xiaoming teaching material remains a practitioner seed and internal synthesis source. It is not promoted to primary TheoryObject authority or independent empirical evidence.

## Research method

Each candidate is compiled through:

`primary/seminal source -> atomic claim -> mechanism -> EvidenceRelation -> boundary/competing explanation -> PIT observable -> proposed hypothesis -> Benchmark Seed`.

EvidenceRelation roles are closed to `supports`, `contradicts`, `boundary`, and `competing_mechanism`. Paper counting is prohibited. Replication gaps are retained explicitly rather than inferred away.

## Batch A｜Reality Mechanics

### QXM1-CAND-01｜Fundamental Driver Decomposition

Verified anchors:

- `QXM2-SRC-LEV-THIAGARAJAN-1993` — fundamental signals can carry information associated with earnings persistence/value relevance; not a universal causal driver ontology.
- `QXM2-SRC-ABARBANELL-BUSHEE-1997` — fundamental signals are empirically related to future earnings changes and analyst forecast revisions, with macro/firm/industry context affecting interpretation; association is not causality.
- `QXM2-SRC-NISSIM-PENMAN-2001` — financial statement analysis can be hierarchical and can separate operating from financing activities; the hierarchy is an analytical architecture rather than causal proof.

QXM2 therefore preserves a two-part proposition: economically meaningful decomposition may add information beyond headline metrics, but the driver graph must be sector-profiled, point-in-time, and benchmarked against simpler revenue/margin/consensus baselines before any forecasting authority is considered.

### QXM1-CAND-02｜Three-Statement Integrity & Cash Conversion

Verified anchors:

- `QXM2-SRC-DECHOW-1994` — accrual accounting can improve performance measurement when cash flows have timing/matching noise. This directly blocks the folk rule that cash flow is always economically superior to earnings.
- `QXM2-SRC-SLOAN-1996` — accrual and cash-flow components contain different information about future earnings persistence in the studied sample; this is not a fraud inference.
- `QXM2-SRC-DECHOW-DICHEV-2002` — accrual estimation error is a measurable dimension of accrual/earnings quality.
- `QXM2-SRC-RICHARDSON-SLOAN-SOLIMAN-TUNA-2005` — accrual reliability differs by balance-sheet category and is associated with earnings persistence.
- `QXM2-SRC-FAIRFIELD-WHISENANT-YOHN-2003` — growth in net operating assets is a competing explanation for some apparent accrual effects; normal investment/growth must be separated from accounting deterioration.
- `QXM2-SRC-IAS7` — IAS 7 is normative reporting authority for operating/investing/financing cash-flow classification and cash reconciliation. It is deliberately excluded from predictive Shadow TheoryObjects.

The resulting QXM2 boundary is permanent: **statement divergence is diagnostic, not proof of manipulation, fraud, insolvency, or future underperformance.**

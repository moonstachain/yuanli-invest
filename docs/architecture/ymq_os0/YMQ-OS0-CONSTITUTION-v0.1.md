# YMQ-OS0｜Macro Quant Constitution v0.1

**Stage:** `YMQ-OS0-G0`  
**Status:** `IMPLEMENTATION_CANDIDATE / NOT_CANON`  
**Parent:** `YIOS0 L2–L8 research side only`  
**Machine contract:** `config/ymq_os0/ymq_os0_contract.v0.1.json`

## 1｜Mission

YMQ-OS is a governed macro-quant research subsystem. Its job is to turn time-valid external Reality into reproducible, defeasible research state while preserving evidence lineage and preventing research authority from leaking into Capital or Execution authority.

It is **not** a parallel Investment OS, portfolio allocator, trading engine, broker gateway, or automatic forecasting authority.

## 2｜Canonical research loop

`External Reality → Evidence Snapshot → PIT Observation → PIT Feature → PIT State → Transmission → Capability Run → Research Settlement → LearningDelta`

Every step must be attributable to versioned evidence/code and remain reproducible from its Point-in-Time boundary.

## 3｜Inherited mother laws

1. `Reality > Belief`.
2. Every intelligence claim carries a pre-registered defeat condition.
3. `ClaimAuthority <= EvidenceAuthority`.
4. `ResearchPass != CapitalPass`.
5. `ResearchAuthority != CapitalAuthority != ExecutionAuthority`.
6. `UNKNOWN = DENY`.
7. `Receipt = Ledger; Status = Projection`.
8. Past Evidence and Settlement are immutable; Learning may revise only future state.

## 4｜Namespace law

Existing `YMQ4-*` identifiers remain historical program identities and are never silently renumbered.

Stable research capability-family roles are:

- `MQE1｜Macro Reality Engine`
- `MQE2｜Industrial Reality Engine`
- `MQE3｜Narrative × Herding Engine`
- `MQE4｜Dynamic Transmission Engine`
- `MQE5｜Price × Payoff Engine`

`YMQ4 != MQE4 Canon`. Existing YMQ4 work is inherited evidence only to the extent of its exact settlement.

## 5｜YMQ4 inherited Reality

- `YMQ4-DP1-A / DP1-B / B2` remain inherited physical evidence.
- `YMQ4-B2` remains a physical baseline proof with `FIXED_BETA_BEATS_NULL`.
- `YMQ4-B3` remains visible as `REALITY_PASS / SCIENTIFIC_NO_GO`, observation `DYNAMIC_BETA_DOES_NOT_BEAT_B2`, PR #72 Draft/Open/Not Merged, and no Canon authority.

A later architecture may learn from a NO-GO; it may not rewrite it into a success.

## 6｜Point-in-Time constitution

For replay boundary `T0`:

`ALLOW(object) iff object.known_as_of <= T0`

Where applicable the system distinguishes event, observation, release, vintage, known-as-of, retrieval, ingestion and settlement time. A later revision cannot silently substitute for an earlier vintage. Historical text without provable contemporaneous availability cannot enter a PIT scientific claim.

## 7｜Settlement law

Research settlement has three independent axes:

- `physical_status`
- `scientific_status`
- `authority_status`

Therefore `PHYSICAL_PASS / SCIENTIFIC_NO_GO` is a valid successful Reality audit. Engineering success is not automatically scientific success, and scientific success is not Capital authority.

## 8｜Provider law

GitHub, Supabase/Postgres, object storage/NAS, Hugging Face, containers, Web, ChatGPT and Notion are physical providers or projections with bounded roles. Provider identity never grants Canon authority.

Compute providers are replaceable. Experience surfaces are projection-only. Execution remains outside YMQ-OS.

## 9｜Explicit non-authorizations

G0 does **not** authorize:

- Supabase/runtime mutation;
- HF repository write;
- YMQ3 physical Reality run;
- portfolio weighting or position sizing;
- VeighNa invocation;
- broker credentials or connection;
- paper or live orders;
- real capital movement;
- automatic Canon promotion from a positive result.

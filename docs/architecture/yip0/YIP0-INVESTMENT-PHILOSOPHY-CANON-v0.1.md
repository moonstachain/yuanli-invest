Six-word compression:

## 2. What YIP0 is solving

The Golden Triangle can easily degrade into a scoring worksheet if its philosophical substrate is left implicit.

YIP0 freezes a deeper answer to four questions:

1. **Ontology｜世界是什么？** — an open, evolving, non-equilibrium process rather than a static state.
2. **Epistemology｜我们能知道什么？** — only provisional, evidence-bounded hypotheses.
3. **Market dynamics｜价格怎样形成？** — through interaction among reality, shared belief, capital, price and feedback.
4. **Decision philosophy｜怎样行动？** — seek asymmetric right tails while preserving the right to continue compounding.

The philosophy is therefore not a substitute for P/N/X/E/V/S. It is their upstream constraint.

## 3. Four mother laws

### `REALITY_OVER_BELIEF | 实在高于叙事`

Reality is not created by research language. Belief can influence reality, but belief does not become truth merely through diffusion, price appreciation or institutional popularity.

This law prevents narrative relativism.

### `REFLEXIVITY | 反身性`

In financial and social systems, beliefs can alter actions; actions can alter capital allocation and price; price and financing conditions can alter later fundamentals.

This law prevents static-fundamental reductionism.

### `TAIL_ASYMMETRY | 尾部非对称`

Investment outcomes are not adequately described by average returns or raw hit-rate. Right-tail concentration, left-tail damage, payoff geometry and path dependence matter.

This law prevents Gaussian / hit-rate reductionism.

### `SURVIVAL_FIRST | 生存优先`

The ability to participate in future opportunities dominates locally optimal expected-return calculations that permit unacceptable ruin risk.

This law prevents ruinous optimization.

## 4. Twelve philosophical axioms

### `YL-PH-01 | Becoming before Being`

> **世界首先是过程，不是状态。**

The first research question is not merely “what is the current state?” but “what is this system becoming?”

Primary mapping: `P | Reality State Transition`.

Operational implication: structural migration, cost curves, adoption, supply/demand, institutions, balance sheets and production functions matter before static snapshot ranking.

---

### `YL-PH-02 | Reality exceeds belief`

> **现实独立存在，但人的认识永远不完备。**

Invariant:

> `P != N`

Narrative strength, attention, price appreciation or institutional consensus cannot establish physical, economic or institutional truth by themselves.

Primary mapping: `P`, `N`, `E`.

Operational implication: every expectation-regime claim must be separable from the underlying reality claim it references.

---

### `YL-PH-03 | All investment knowledge is provisional`

> **所有投资判断都是暂时假说。**

No object becomes infallible because it is called Canon, Gold, benchmarked, model-generated or widely accepted.

Primary mapping: `E`, learning loop.

Operational implication: every material claim should carry evidence authority, falsifier and revision rule. Canon is governed current knowledge, not final truth.

---

### `YL-PH-04 | Belief is an economic state variable`

> **信念不是噪声，而是金融系统的真实状态变量。**

Shared belief can change spending, financing, hiring, capex, portfolio allocation, valuation and institutional behavior.

Primary mapping: `N | Expectation Regime`.

Operational implication: N is not reduced to raw sentiment. Research concerns story, diffusion, expectation translation, crowding and regime break.

---

### `YL-PH-05 | Belief can change reality`

> **信念既反映现实，也能改变现实。**

Financial systems can contain the loop:

`Reality -> Belief -> Action/Capital -> Price -> Financing/Incentives -> later Reality`

Primary mapping: interaction among `P`, `N`, `V`.

Operational implication: research must identify when a belief is merely descriptive versus when it is altering financing conditions, collateral, capex, demand or institutional behavior.

---

### `YL-PH-06 | Price is both outcome and cause`

> **价格既是结果，也是原因。**

Price can affect financing capacity, collateral, employee incentives, acquisition currency, investor attention and future actions.

Primary mapping: `N`, `V`, reflexive-loop analysis.

Operational implication: price must not be modeled only as a passive terminal output when the target system contains price-sensitive feedback.

---

### `YL-PH-07 | Non-equilibrium can persist`

> **市场没有义务迅速回到“合理价格”。**

A valuation gap does not imply an immediate mean-reversion trade. Positive feedback can enlarge deviations before any later break.

Primary mapping: `N`, `V`, `Xa`.

Operational implication: every reflexive thesis should distinguish the sustaining loop from its `Loop Breaker`; “too expensive” and “too cheap” are insufficient standalone timing claims.

---

### `YL-PH-08 | Returns are non-Gaussian`

> **金融收益是肥尾的，财富高度集中于少数极端结果。**

Average returns cannot fully describe long-horizon wealth creation or destruction.

Primary mapping: `X | Structural Asymmetry`.

Operational implication: ResearchCapability design must be able to represent tail concentration, discontinuity, skew and asymmetric damage without collapsing them into one master score.

---

### `YL-PH-09 | Payoff dominates raw hit-rate`

> **赔率与收益结构的重要性高于单纯预测准确率。**

Decision quality depends on probability, payoff geometry, path, liquidity and ruin exposure — not raw classification accuracy alone.

Primary mapping: `Xs`, `Xa`, `Xp`.

Operational implication:

- `Xs` asks where asymmetry comes from;
- `Xa` asks when a tail is conditionally activating;
- `Xp` asks whether the chosen instrument can capture it.

Canonical invariant remains:

> `X := (Xs, Xa, Xp)`

This tuple is structural decomposition, not addition or multiplication.

---

### `YL-PH-10 | Great future != great investment`

> **伟大的未来，不等于伟大的投资。**

Price is an admissibility boundary. A correct structural thesis can still produce a poor investment if too much future is already embedded in the current price.

Primary mapping: `V | Price-Implied Expectations`.

Operational implication: V asks what future the current price requires; target price and canonical upside percentage are not the ontology of V.

---

### `YL-PH-11 | Survival precedes optimization`

> **生存优先于优化。**

The right to continue compounding dominates local optimization that can cause unacceptable ruin.

Primary mapping: `S | Portfolio Survival`.

Operational implication: concentration, leverage, stress correlation, liquidity, funding, gap risk and path dependency must be evaluated separately from issuer quality. S does not automatically output a recommended position size.

---

### `YL-PH-12 | Reality has final settlement authority`

> **现实拥有最终裁决权。**

Models, narratives and Canon objects must answer to later reality.

Primary mapping: learning loop.

Operational implication:

`Theory -> Hypothesis -> Capability -> Runtime -> Replay -> Benchmark -> Failure -> Future Settlement -> Capability Revision`

Failure and falsification are information. A system that cannot demote its own prior knowledge violates YIP0.

## 6. Force redefined philosophically

`Force` is not a scalar ontology.

The philosophical meaning of Force is a **coherent directional field** in which:

- reality is undergoing structural transition;
- belief is diffusing or repricing that transition;
- value capture is structurally asymmetric;
- evidence remains admissible;
- price has not destroyed the payoff;
- survival remains intact.

Forbidden:

- `Force Score = 87`
- weighted P/N/X/E/V/S master score
- using popularity as proof of P
- using P correctness to bypass V
- using right-tail potential to bypass S

The highest-interest research state is often not maximum consensus. It can be a temporal gap:

> **Reality has changed; structural value concentration is emerging; belief and price have not fully caught up.**

This is a philosophical description of potential Alpha, not an automatic trade signal.

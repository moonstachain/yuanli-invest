## 2 Ontology distinctions

Canonical inequality:

```text
asset_form != pricing_archetype != return_engine != engine_thesis != position_expression
```

The distinctions are binding:

- `asset_form` describes what the research target legally/economically is.
- `pricing_archetype` describes dominant pricing physics.
- `return_engine` describes the mechanism expected to generate investor return.
- `engine_thesis` is a governed interpretation of one target through one primary return mechanism.
- `position_expression` is the capital/instrument expression of a thesis under separate survival authority.

A research target is not a thesis. Target identity must not determine thesis identity. The same target may host multiple valid engine theses with different horizons, evidence, falsifiers and price semantics. Book membership is thesis/position-specific, not ticker-specific.

## 4 Genesis Engine Set

C/R/X are the **Genesis Engine Set**, not a proven exhaustive universal ontology. The engine registry is open-world. Future engines may be added only through separately governed, evidence-backed successor work.

### `ENG-C | Compounding`

Stable question:

> Is the primary source of expected return durable economic value creation and high-return reinvestment that compounds through time, purchased at an admissible price?

Primary source of return:

- durable value-pool growth;
- value capture and economic control;
- ROIC / cash conversion / balance-sheet resilience;
- reinvestment runway;
- compounding duration.

Price semantics: long-horizon expected return and implied operating assumptions. Re-rating may affect realized return, but C cannot depend on re-rating as its primary source-of-return claim.

Not C by itself: a generic good-company label, a hot growth stock, narrative strength, short-term multiple expansion or the fact an asset can rise substantially.

### `ENG-R | Reflexive Repricing`

Stable question:

> Is the primary source of expected return a finite-horizon repricing process in which belief, capital flow, price, positioning and later reality reinforce or break one another?

Primary source of return:

- change in market-implied expectations / discounting;
- liquidity, capital-flow and positioning feedback;
- narrative / expectation diffusion;
- earnings or reality realization that validates or breaks the loop;
- leadership, breadth and price confirmation of propagation.

Price semantics: price is both sensor and participant in the reflexive loop. Cheap/expensive alone is not a sufficient timing rule.

Not R by itself: all price movement, generic momentum, macro regime classification, narrative alone or evidence-free sentiment.

`ENG-R` is a return-engine namespace identity and must never alias or redefine historical `CAP-R-01 | Regime Causal Decomposition`.

### `ENG-X | Convexity`

Stable question:

> Is the primary source of expected portfolio payoff a deliberately asymmetric or nonlinear payoff structure in which conditional tail activation and expression geometry dominate the result?

Primary source of return:

- conditional tail activation;
- optionality and nonlinear payout;
- explicit payoff asymmetry;
- volatility / skew / path / time structure;
- bounded or deliberately engineered left tail relative to open right-tail opportunity where the instrument permits.

Canonical conceptual components:

- `Xa | Tail Activation`;
- `Xp | Payoff Geometry`.

Price semantics: premium, implied distribution, volatility, skew, carry/decay, breakeven and the cost of obtaining convexity.

Not X by itself: every monopoly, network effect, high-growth company, an asset that can rise a lot, or durable value capture without an explicit convex payoff mechanism.

## 7 No Silent Thesis Migration

Canonical invariant:

> `NO_SILENT_THESIS_MIGRATION`

A losing R trade cannot silently become a C investment. A decaying X expression cannot be rescued by an unrelated long-horizon P thesis. A C thesis cannot be automatically liquidated solely because a tactical R state changes.

Any engine change requires a future governed event with explicit destination-engine evidence, horizon, source of return, falsifiers and authority. The planned identity is `AssetGraduationEvent`; implementation is deferred beyond ME0.

The important handoff case is `R -> C`: a narrative/mainline leader may graduate to a Compounder candidate only after reality, value capture, ROIC/FCF, reinvestment and durability are separately evidenced.

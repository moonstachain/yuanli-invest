# R2 Source Boundary Map v0.1

R2 is a capability-specification pack, not a claim that every ancestor theory has been independently re-proven inside this repository. Source class and claim strength must remain explicit.

| Source class | R2 examples | Permitted use | Prohibited overclaim |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary source verified in supplied evidence | Shiller (2017), `Narrative Economics`, uploaded NBER WP 23075 | Direct theory/mechanism grounding for narrative-diffusion hypotheses, with Shiller's own causal cautions preserved | “Narrative mechanically determines price” or “SIR is literally the market” |
| Internal candidate theory lineage | Dosi, Perez, Rosen, Katz-Shapiro, Arthur, Bessembinder, EVT, Black-Scholes-Merton, Roy, Kelly, Breiman, Merton | Define candidate lineage and falsifiable R2 hypotheses while marked conservatively `survey_only` until primary receipts are installed | Claiming primary-source verification merely because the internal lineage cites the paper |
| User analytical synthesis | Narrative-economics AI reports; Perez × Shiller three-cycle reports; `构建原力投研方法论.txt` | Preserve Yuanli terminology, scientific boundaries, historical analogies and research questions | Treating historical analogy as causal proof or current market facts as timeless canon |
| Practitioner claim | Xu Jiancheng 2026 causal-AI deck/interview notes | Preserve the research question: leading extreme factors, direction/strength/lag, conditional extreme probability and impact | Treating self-reported accuracy, returns, institutional-client outcomes or causal identification as verified evidence |

## R2 evidence posture

R2 maturity is `specified`, so primary-source completeness is not a claim of benchmark validity. Before any capability can become `implemented` or later:

1. reference implementation must exist and be deterministic where required;
2. the exact source/version and field lineage used by the implementation must be pinned;
3. benchmark execution must use PIT/OOS rules;
4. practitioner claims must not be used as benchmark ground truth without independent verification.

## Current-market facts

R2 does not require today's market levels, prices, company valuations or portfolio holdings to define these 12 capabilities. Such time-varying facts belong to runtime evidence, not to the research-capability canon. This separation prevents current-state narratives from contaminating the reusable method object.

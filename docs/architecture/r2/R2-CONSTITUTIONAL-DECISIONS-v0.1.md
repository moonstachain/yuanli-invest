# R2 Constitutional Decisions v0.1

This document records the Human Review constitutional layer for **R2 | PNX-S Gold Capability Pack**. It intentionally separates human meaning decisions from machine-verifiable repository facts.

## Accepted constitutional questions

### Q1 | Capability Ontology — ACCEPTED

`ResearchCapability` is the center object and the compounding research asset. Reports, opinions, stock lists, prompts and Agents are outputs, views or runtimes; they are not the durable canonical unit.

### Q2 | Gold Primitive Set — ACCEPTED

The exact 12 R0 seed capability IDs are accepted as the first Gold research primitives. `Gold` means strategic priority plus `maturity_state=specified`. It does not mean implemented, benchmark-passed, shadow-qualified or canon.

### Q3 | PNX-S Constitution — ACCEPTED

Canonical semantics:

```text
P = Reality / Direction
N = Belief / Timing
X := (Xs, Xa, Xp)
V = Price / Strike
S = Survival Outer Ring
```

`X := (Xs, Xa, Xp)` is a structural decomposition. Arithmetic addition or multiplication is not canonical semantics. Scalar PNX / Force scoring is prohibited as canonical ontology.

### Q4 | Epistemic Constitution — ACCEPTED

Claim authority cannot exceed evidence authority. Primary evidence, research synthesis, practitioner claims and hypotheses remain distinguishable. A fluent synthesis does not upgrade source status.

### Q5 | Falsification Constitution — ACCEPTED

Capability promotion requires, where applicable: point-in-time discipline, OOS/walk-forward evaluation, calibration, false-alarm accounting, regime holdout, simpler baselines, multiple-testing control and complexity penalties. Benchmark beauty cannot substitute for falsifiability.

### Q6 | Investment Boundary — ACCEPTED

PNX identifies opportunity structure; V governs the price/entry condition; S governs the right to remain solvent and continue compounding. Research Canon does not automatically produce target prices, buy/sell actions, portfolio weights, recommended position sizes or execution authority.

### Q7 | Runtime Sovereignty — ACCEPTED

ResearchCapability and CanonicalDataField must remain provider-independent. Wind AI, Codex and future data/model vendors are Runtime / Adapter layers, not the methodology ontology.

## Machine-verifiable invariants

The following remain mandatory but are not constitutional questions for Human Review:

- Registry counts, IDs, cross-object references and schema validity must be machine-validated.
- `registry/providers/` remains empty in R2; provider-specific Wind/Codex contracts belong to R3.
- Q1 / A6 / M1.2 state, A9 operational-canon authority, Evidence/Outcome admission and RSI FROZEN state remain unchanged unless independently authorized.

# YMA55-H4.2｜Same-Case Baseline Benchmark & H1/H2/H3 Ablation

## Executive settlement

H4.2 asks a narrower and more scientific question than H4.1:

> On the same 12 historical cases, with the same PIT evidence and the same post-resolution settlements, what incremental epistemic value is visible as we add evidence gating, competing mechanisms, and the full H1/H2 resolver discipline?

The answer is asymmetric.

The strongest measured increment comes from **competing-mechanism adjudication**. Evidence gating adds an important safety benefit by permitting abstention when the evidence pack is incomplete. The full resolver's falsifier/breaker/coverage discipline adds a smaller but positive increment. H3's transferability and prior-violation contribution cannot be identified in this historical-only experiment and must remain unresolved until a paired current-world transferability challenge exists.

This report does **not** claim exact replication of the pre-H1/H2/H3 YMA55 system. `B0_NAIVE_PRIMARY_PRIOR` is a deliberately simple frozen baseline, not a reconstruction of every behavior of the old framework.

## 1. Experimental design

The experiment keeps fixed:

- exactly 12 cases;
- the same opaque blind case identities;
- the same T0 evidence packets;
- the same hydration status;
- the same settlement mechanism adjudications.

Only the resolver discipline changes.

Five frozen variants are compared:

| Variant | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| B0 | Naive primary prior; always resolves to Primary and does not claim to replicate old YMA55 |
| B1 | Evidence-gated primary prior; can abstain on incomplete hydration but still cannot compare mechanisms |
| B2 | Primary/Alternative/Null competition using observables and required conditions, without falsifier/breaker penalties |
| B3 | H1/H2 full blind resolver with competing mechanisms, evidence coverage, falsifiers and breakers |
| B4 | H1/H2 resolver with H3 architecture present, but H3 contribution deliberately marked unidentifiable in historical-only replay |

No variant has capital or trading authority.

## 2. Machine benchmark results

The dedicated H4.2 validator on repository-gates run #470 produced:

| Variant | Fully hydrated mechanism accuracy | Hard-negative discrimination | Matches | Mismatches | Abstentions | Forced partial case? |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| B0 Naive Primary | 3/11 | 0/8 | 4 | 8 | 0 | **Yes** |
| B1 Evidence-Gated Primary | 3/11 | 0/8 | 3 | 8 | 1 | No |
| B2 Competing / No Breaker | 9/10 | 6/8 | 9 | 1 | 2 | No |
| B3 H1+H2 Full Resolver | **10/11** | **7/8** | **10** | **1** | 1 | No |

The `9/10` denominator for B2 is not a typo. B2 abstains/ties on one additional fully hydrated case, leaving ten fully hydrated cases with a directional mechanism resolution.

## 3. What the evidence gate actually contributes

B0 and B1 have the same fully hydrated mechanism accuracy: `3/11`.

Therefore this experiment does not support the claim that evidence gating alone improves mechanism discrimination.

Its value is different and important:

- B0 forcibly resolves the partially hydrated 1971 Gold case to Primary;
- B1 refuses to resolve it and outputs insufficient evidence;
- the unsafe forced-resolution flag changes from `true` to `false`.

So the correct conclusion is:

> **Evidence gating improves epistemic safety by creating a legitimate abstention state; it does not, by itself, solve causal mechanism discrimination.**

This is a useful result because it separates “knowing when not to know” from “knowing which mechanism is right.”

## 4. The dominant measured increment: competing mechanisms

The largest change occurs between primary-only baselines and B2.

B1:

- fully hydrated mechanism accuracy `3/11`;
- hard-negative discrimination `0/8`.

B2:

- mechanism accuracy `9/10` among resolved fully hydrated cases;
- hard-negative discrimination `6/8`;
- one mechanism mismatch;
- two abstentions.

The large gain is generated by allowing the Primary thesis to lose to an Alternative or Null under the same evidence.

This is the strongest evidence so far for H2's core proposition:

> A macro research system becomes materially less self-confirming when every thesis is forced to compete with explicit alternatives rather than merely accumulating supporting evidence for the original story.

The result should still be described as **within this constructed challenge set**, not as a population estimate.

## 5. What falsifier / breaker / coverage discipline adds

B2 removes explicit falsifier/breaker penalties. B3 restores the full H1/H2 resolver discipline.

The result moves from:

- `9/10` resolved fully hydrated mechanism accuracy and `6/8` hard-negative discrimination;

to:

- `10/11` fully hydrated mechanism accuracy and `7/8` hard-negative discrimination.

At the same time, the extra fully hydrated abstention in B2 is resolved correctly by B3.

This is a smaller increment than the move from B1 to B2, but it is directionally positive in this challenge set.

Therefore the defensible statement is:

> **Competing mechanisms create the dominant measured gain; falsifier/breaker/coverage logic supplies additional discrimination and reduces unnecessary unresolved cases.**

## 6. H1 contribution is only partially isolated

The experiment should not pretend that H1 is cleanly measured as an independent scalar contribution.

The five-layer kernel is present in the case construction, hypothesis packets, and full resolver, but B0/B1 are not an exact “H1 off / H1 on” pair. They also differ in evidence-gating semantics and do not recreate a pre-H1 ontology.

Therefore:

`H1 individual contribution = PARTIALLY_IDENTIFIED_NOT_CLEANLY_ISOLATED`

The current evidence supports H1 as a useful compression and object-separation architecture, but does not assign it an independent numerical effect size.

## 7. H3 does not move the historical-only score — and that is the correct result

B3 and B4 are intentionally identical in this experiment:

- B3: `10/11`, hard negative `7/8`;
- B4: `10/11`, hard negative `7/8`.

This is not evidence that H3 has no value.

H3 concerns whether historical priors are structurally transferable into another regime and whether current reality violates those priors. A replay that only adjudicates historical mechanism identity, without a paired target regime/current-world evidence stream, cannot legitimately measure that contribution.

Therefore:

`H3 incremental contribution = NOT_IDENTIFIABLE_IN_HISTORICAL_ONLY_REPLAY`

Any attempt to manufacture an H3 uplift inside this dataset would be methodologically invalid.

## 8. Critical failures remain visible

### 2020 Gold

The B3/B4 resolver still selects the scarcity/monetary-expansion Primary while settlement favors the real-rate/liquidity Alternative.

This remains the major mechanism miss and is not averaged away.

### 1971 Gold

Evidence-gated variants continue to abstain because the T0 pack does not satisfy the independent-E1 threshold.

The model receives no credit for the fact that the positive-pattern thesis looks convincing in hindsight.

These two cases are valuable because they demonstrate two different epistemic disciplines:

- wrong mechanism must remain a recorded miss;
- insufficient evidence must remain an abstention.

## 9. What H4.2 establishes

Within this controlled 12-case challenge set, the evidence supports:

1. **incremental superiority of the full mechanism-discrimination resolver versus the frozen naive-primary and evidence-gated-primary baselines;**
2. **strong incremental contribution from explicit competing mechanisms;**
3. **a smaller positive contribution from falsifier/breaker/coverage discipline;**
4. **a distinct safety contribution from evidence gating through abstention.**

These findings justify the narrower statement:

> YMA55 2.1's mechanism-competition architecture materially reduces primary-thesis anchoring and improves hard-negative discrimination relative to the simple frozen baselines on the same cases and evidence.

## 10. What H4.2 does not establish

The following remain unproven:

- exact superiority versus a faithful executable replica of the pre-H1/H2/H3 YMA55 framework;
- out-of-sample forecasting superiority;
- a statistically generalizable 10/11 success probability;
- an independent numerical contribution for H1;
- any incremental contribution for H3 in a current-world transfer task;
- Historical Gold admission;
- Canon promotion;
- Portfolio, sizing, signal, or trading authority.

The 12 cases are a deliberately constructed diagnostic challenge set, not an IID sample.

## 11. Strategic interpretation

H4.2 changes the interpretation of the YMA55 evolution.

The most valuable upgrade is not simply the five-layer diagram, nor a larger historical database. The measured core is:

> **Force the favored mechanism to lose when another mechanism explains the observable transmission better.**

The architecture can therefore be understood as three different epistemic functions:

- **Evidence Gate:** prevents unsupported certainty;
- **Competing Mechanisms:** attacks causal self-confirmation;
- **Falsifier / Breaker discipline:** improves discrimination when evidence paths conflict.

H3 remains a separate question about whether a historically valid prior deserves authority in the present.

## 12. Authority freeze

H4.2 grants no authority for:

- Historical Gold admission;
- Constitution mutation;
- Engine Registry mutation;
- Canon promotion;
- Portfolio allocation;
- position sizing;
- buy/sell/hold signals;
- live execution;
- merge.

`Research PASS != Capital PASS` remains binding.

## 13. Recommended next gate — not authorized here

`YMA55-H4.3｜H3 Transferability & Prior-Violation Paired Challenge`

The next scientifically necessary question is now very specific:

> When a historically valid mechanism is transported into a structurally different target regime, can H3 correctly downgrade non-transferable priors and detect current-world violations without simply fitting hindsight?

That experiment should use paired source-regime / target-regime cases, pre-freeze transferability judgments, and current-evidence streams. Until then, H3's incremental contribution remains unresolved.

# Yuanli Investment Research Intelligence Constitution vNext

## 1. Mission

`yuanli-invest` is the **Yuanli Investment Research Intelligence Canon | 原力投研智能正典**.

Its mission is:

> **Compile investment knowledge into reality-tested, machine-callable research intelligence.**

The durable compounding unit is a versioned `ResearchCapability`, not a report, stock list, prompt, model vendor, Agent or data provider.

The base OS identity remains:

> **一核 · 三界 · 三门 · 一环**
>
> `one_core_three_worlds_three_gates_one_loop`

R2.3-A.1 hardens this accepted backbone for cross-asset research. It does not add a fourth human world.

## 2. 一核 | Right-Tail Compounding

Objective:

> **Lifetime Right-Tail Capture under Survival Constraints**

Human mnemonic:

> **抓住右尾 · 让赢家复利 · 永不出局**

This is not an arithmetic formula.

## 3. 三界 | Three Worlds

Human front-end:

> **势 · 信 · 极**

### P | 势 | Reality State Transition

Human umbrella question:

> **现实世界正在往哪里运动？**

P contains two human-facing subspaces:

- `P.capital | 资本势`: growth, inflation, liquidity, risk appetite, term premium/duration supply, funding stress and policy reaction function.
- `P.asset | 资产势`: asset-/industry-specific physical or institutional reality such as technology/adoption, supply-demand/inventory/cost curve, cash flow/balance sheet, fiscal/monetary/regulatory structures and balance-of-payments/reserve dynamics where relevant.

`R | Regime Causal Decomposition` is a separately typed machine decomposition/context for `P.capital` because its observables, horizons and authorization effects differ from asset-specific reality.

**R is not a fourth human world and does not replace P.**

R may constrain downstream transferability or execution authorization, but a hostile regime does not automatically make valid bottom-up research false.

### N | 信 | Expectation Regime

Human question:

> **市场正在为哪个未来付钱？**

N is not raw sentiment. Canonical expectation chain:

`story -> diffusion -> expectation translation -> crowding -> regime break`

N authority is routed by the dominant pricing archetype and may be `binding`, `auxiliary`, or `irrelevant`.

Narrative strength is not truth. A narrative/expectation break triggers bounded V/Xa/Xp re-underwriting; it is not an automatic trading instruction.

### X | 极 | Structural Asymmetry

Canonical semantics:

> `X := (Xs, Xa, Xp)`

This is a structural tuple, not addition or numeric multiplication.

#### Xs | Structural Asymmetry Source

Universal question:

> **为什么这里会产生不成比例的收益或风险？**

`Xs` is the mother concept. Asset-specific implementations may include:

- equity -> Value Control Point / network / scale / switching cost / bottleneck / distribution;
- commodity -> scarcity / inventory criticality / supply elasticity / capacity lag;
- sovereign rates -> duration / convexity / term-premium or policy-path asymmetry;
- credit -> default / spread / recovery / refinancing asymmetry;
- FX -> policy divergence / carry / balance-of-payments / positioning / flow;
- gold / monetary asset -> monetary scarcity / real-rate sensitivity / reserve demand / credibility regime;
- derivatives -> volatility mispricing / convexity / skew / path dependence;
- crypto -> network / scarcity / liquidity reflexivity / protocol-control mechanisms.

**Value Control Point is an equity-specialized implementation, not the universal ontology of Xs.**

#### Xa | Asymmetry Activation / Conditional Tail State

Question:

> **为什么是现在？哪一侧尾部正在变肥？**

Xa is a conditional tail state. It must not be presented as a calibrated probability-of-being-right unless explicit calibration exists.

#### Xp | Asymmetry Capture / Payoff Geometry

Question:

> **用什么工具才能真正吃到这个不对称？**

Underlying right-tail potential does not imply attractive instrument payoff.

**X semantic indivisibility rule:** Research OS / Portfolio OS separation is a governance-authority split only. It does not partition or redefine `X := (Xs, Xa, Xp)`.

## 4. 三门 | Investment Admissibility Constitution

Human front-end:

> **真 · 价 · 生**

### E | 真 | Evidence Gate

E is horizontal across every research node.

Invariant:

> **Claim Authority <= Evidence Authority**

Primary evidence, independent empirical evidence, research synthesis, practitioner claim and hypothesis must remain distinguishable.

Every material claim should be able to carry:
`source / as_of / claim_type / evidence_authority / confidence / falsifier / revision_rule`.

### V | 价 | Price Gate

Question:

> **这个未来已经被当前价格预付了多少？**

Canonical V identity is `Price-Implied Expectations`. Different asset forms may use replaceable algorithms such as Reverse DCF, implied policy path, futures curve, forward/carry, real-rate/monetary-premium decomposition or implied-volatility surfaces.

Target price and upside percentage are not canonical V state.

### S | 生 | Survival Gate

Question:

> **如果错了，组合还有没有下一局？**

`Issuer Durability != Portfolio Survival`.

S concerns concentration, stress correlation, leverage, liquidity, funding, gap risk, path dependency and capital resilience. It does not automatically produce recommended position size.

## 5. Human Navigation

The primary human interface is frozen as:

> **势 · 信 · 极｜真 · 价 · 生**
>
> **顺大势 · 乘共识 · 押极值｜凭真据 · 买好价 · 永不死**

The accepted Seven Questions remain a deeper diagnostic projection:

1. 世界真的在变吗？ -> P
2. 不对称从哪里来？ -> Xs
3. 市场正在为哪个未来付钱？ -> N
4. 这个未来已经被价格预付多少？ -> V
5. 哪一侧尾部正在被激活？ -> Xa
6. 我的工具能否真正捕获右尾？ -> Xp
7. 如果我错了，还能继续复利吗？ -> S

E remains horizontal.

Machine complexity (`R / A0 / A1 / L0-L4 / typed ResearchStateVector`) remains backstage and must not replace this human mnemonic.

## 6. L0-L4 | Cross-Asset Authority Ladder

- `L0 | Cross-Asset Regime`: equity / sovereign rates / credit / commodities / FX / monetary assets / cash.
- `L1 | Asset-Class Regime`: the active sub-regime inside the selected asset class.
- `L2 | Theme / Macro-Theme Regime`: theme expansion, internal rotation or external de-risking.
- `L3 | Value Pool / Market Structure`: where value, scarcity, duration, carry or control is concentrated.
- `L4 | Asset / Instrument`: current asset state and instrument geometry.

Invariant:

> **Lower-level truth does not imply higher-level authorization.**

This separates research validity from capital permission.

## 7. A | Two-Stage Asset / Pricing Router

A is routing infrastructure, not a score and not a sector taxonomy.

### A0 | asset_form

`equity / sovereign_rates / credit / commodity / FX / monetary_asset / crypto / derivative`

### A1 | pricing_archetype

`growth / compounder / cyclical / duration / scarcity / event_optionality`

Invariant:

> **Asset form is not pricing model.**

The same target may require more than one pricing archetype. A1 routes the dominant pricing engine and N authority.

## 8. Research State, Not Scalar Score

Canonical research state is multidimensional.

Prohibited as ontology:

- scalar PNX / Force / macro score;
- weighted averages of P/N/X/E/V/S/R;
- arithmetic `Xs + Xa + Xp`;
- collapsing `P.capital` and `P.asset` into one number;
- pretending all dimensions share one time horizon or measurement scale.

Human labels are derived projections only.

## 9. Time-Scale Separation

Different layers may use different horizons:

- P.capital / R: current-to-cyclical;
- P.asset / Xs: structural, often multi-year;
- N: weeks to years;
- Xa: conditional, often days to months;
- V: current price versus embedded future;
- Xp: instrument lifecycle;
- S: continuous portfolio constraint.

## 10. Research OS vs Portfolio OS

This is a **governance-authority split only**, not an ontology split.

Research OS owns typed research-state production and may study all X semantics.

Portfolio OS owns portfolio aggregation, expression, survival, rebalance and execution-adjacent authority.

Canonical X remains indivisible:

> `X := (Xs, Xa, Xp)`

Research OS does not emit target prices, recommended weights, position sizes or live actions merely because it studies Xp/S.

## 13. Falsification and Cross-Asset Stress

Architecture must survive semantic/routing checks across materially different targets such as NVIDIA, UST30Y, Copper, Gold and USDJPY without forcing one asset's physical model onto another.

Capability promotion later requires point-in-time replay, simpler baselines, failure cases, calibration where applicable, false-alarm accounting, regime holdout, future settlement and revision rules.

Complexity has no natural authority.

## 14. Non-Authorization Boundary

This Constitution does not authorize:

- buy/sell actions;
- target prices;
- recommended portfolio weights;
- position sizing;
- broker/live execution;
- Evidence or Outcome admission;
- benchmark result acceptance merely from documentation;
- A9 operational-canon switch;
- RSI promotion.

`canon` means accepted research capability, not trading authority.

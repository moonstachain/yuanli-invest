# Yuanli Investment Research Intelligence Constitution vNext

## 1. Mission

`yuanli-invest` is the **Yuanli Investment Research Intelligence Canon | 原力投研智能正典**.

Its mission is:

> **Compile investment knowledge into reality-tested, machine-callable research intelligence.**

The durable asset is not a report, stock list, prompt, model vendor, Agent, or data provider. The durable unit is a versioned `ResearchCapability` whose theory, evidence boundary, observable semantics, algorithm lineage, benchmark discipline, failure regimes and runtime history can compound over time.

## 2. 一核 | Right-Tail Compounding

The system optimizes for:

> **Lifetime Right-Tail Capture under Survival Constraints**

Human mnemonic:

> **抓住右尾 · 让赢家复利 · 永不出局**

This is not an arithmetic formula. It is a conditional objective: right-tail opportunity matters only if winners can compound and the portfolio retains the right to participate in future opportunities.

## 3. 三界 | Three Worlds

### P | Reality

`P` studies **Structural Reality Transition**: technology, cost curves, adoption, infrastructure, capital formation, productivity and monetization.

Question: **世界真的在变吗？**

### N | Belief

`N` studies **Belief Diffusion Dynamics**: attention, diffusion, velocity, acceleration, saturation, counter-narratives and reflexive feedback.

Question: **人们正在如何相信？**

Narrative strength is not truth. Narrative causality must not be assumed merely from correlation.

### X | Asymmetry

Canonical semantics:

`X := (Xs, Xa, Xp)`

This is a structural decomposition, not addition and not numeric multiplication.

- `Xs | Asymmetry Source / Structural Value Capture`: why value may concentrate in a small number of winners.
- `Xa | Asymmetry Activation / Conditional Tail State`: when right- or left-tail probabilities change conditionally on state.
- `Xp | Asymmetry Capture / Payoff Geometry`: how an instrument converts the underlying distribution into investor payoff.

Questions:
- **谁会拿走最多价值？**
- **尾部概率正在如何变化？**
- **我的工具能否真正捕获右尾？**

## 4. 三门 | Investment Admissibility Constitution

### E | Evidence Gate

`E` is horizontal across every research node.

Invariant:

> **Claim Authority <= Evidence Authority**

Primary evidence, independent empirical evidence, research synthesis, practitioner claim and hypothesis must remain distinguishable. Fluent synthesis never upgrades evidence status.

Every material claim should be able to carry:
`source / as_of / claim_type / evidence_authority / confidence / falsifier / revision_rule`.

### V | Price Gate

`V` is **Price / Strike / Price of Optionality**.

Question:

> **当前价格已经预付了多少未来？**

Great company does not imply great investment. Canonical V research should prefer stable research questions such as `Price-Implied Expectations`; implementations such as Reverse DCF are algorithms, not eternal ontology.

### S | Survival Gate

`S` is **Portfolio Survival / Right to Continue Compounding**.

`Issuer Durability != Portfolio Survival`.

S concerns ruin, drawdown, leverage, liquidity, correlation and capital resilience. It is not an issuer property and it does not automatically produce recommended position size.

## 5. Research State, Not Scalar Score

Canonical research state is multidimensional.

Prohibited as ontology:
- scalar PNX / Force score;
- weighted averages of P/N/X/V/S;
- arithmetic `Xs + Xa + Xp`;
- pretending all dimensions share one time horizon or one measurement scale.

Human-facing labels such as `golden_extreme`, `latent_dragon`, `paradigm_bubble` and `meme_extreme` may exist only as **projections** from underlying state, never as the canonical state itself.

## 6. Time-Scale Separation

Different research worlds may use different horizons:
- P / Xs: structural, often multi-year;
- N: diffusion, weeks to years;
- Xa: conditional tails, days to months;
- V: current price versus long-horizon embedded expectations;
- Xp: instrument lifecycle;
- S: continuous portfolio constraint.

A single black-box unified horizon is not required and must not be silently imposed.

## 7. Runtime Sovereignty

Canonical meaning belongs to Yuanli. Provider-specific implementation belongs to adapters.

- GitHub Canon: methodology, contracts, registries, reference code, benchmark law, receipts.
- Wind AI: Market Reality / Evidence & Data Runtime.
- Codex: Research Engineering Runtime.
- `quant-workspace`: Reference Quant Runtime; current A9 operational canon unless separately switched.
- Evidence Vault: Audit / Replay Evidence Layer.
- `yuanli-invest-rsi`: Capability Challenger Lane, still governed and not allowed to invent philosophy.

Provider-specific field names must not define Factor or Algorithm semantics.

## 8. Falsification

Capability promotion requires, where applicable:
- point-in-time discipline;
- walk-forward / OOS evaluation;
- calibration;
- false-alarm accounting;
- regime holdout;
- simpler baselines;
- multiple-testing control;
- complexity penalties.

Complexity has no natural authority.

## 9. Non-Authorization Boundary

This Constitution does not authorize:
- buy/sell actions;
- target prices;
- recommended portfolio weights;
- position sizing;
- broker/live execution;
- Evidence or Outcome admission;
- A9 operational-canon switch;
- RSI promotion.

`canon` means accepted research capability, not trading authority.

# 原力七问 | Yuanli Seven Questions

The primary human interface is now:

> **势 · 信 · 极｜真 · 价 · 生**

The Seven Questions remain the deeper diagnostic drilldown. They do not create extra worlds or scores.

## 原力七问

1. **世界真的在变吗？** → `P | 势`
2. **不对称从哪里来？** → `Xs | Structural Asymmetry Source`
3. **市场正在为哪个未来付钱？** → `N | 信`
4. **这个未来已经被价格预付了多少？** → `V | 价`
5. **哪一侧尾部正在被激活？** → `Xa`
6. **我的工具能否真正捕获右尾？** → `Xp`
7. **如果我错了，还能继续复利吗？** → `S | 生`

Above all seven questions sits E:

> **你的证据是什么？什么会证明你错？**

## P drilldown

P contains two human-facing subspaces:

- `P.capital`: money, discount rates, liquidity, risk appetite, funding and policy reaction.
- `P.asset`: asset-specific physical/institutional reality.

`R | Regime Causal Decomposition` is a machine decomposition/context for `P.capital`, **not a fourth human world**.

## Xs routing

Xs is not universally “谁会拿走最多价值”. Its universal question is:

> **为什么这里会产生不成比例的收益或风险？**

For equities this may be Value Control Point; for commodities scarcity/supply elasticity; for rates duration/convexity; for FX policy divergence/carry/flow; for gold monetary scarcity/reserve demand.

## Machine routing

`A0 | asset_form` identifies the market object.

`A1 | pricing_archetype` identifies the binding pricing physics.

Invariant:

> **Asset form is not pricing model.**

The machine Canon uses typed state and evidence contracts rather than a scalar score. Research conclusions are not trading authorization.

## 4. Ambiguity check

PASS WITH NOTES.

### Note A｜“非理性痴迷” remains deliberately human-facing

The term is intentionally preserved for Yuanli brand continuity, but the machine-level object is `Persistent Intrinsic Pull`. Future implementations must not equate the popular phrase with psychiatric obsession, addiction, compulsivity, or a validated clinical construct.

### Note B｜ForceValueDomain is not a score

The intersection should be treated as a structured candidate state, not a global personality score or ranked human-value score.

### Note C｜EAA benchmark design is unresolved by design

The constitution requires a reference group, but does not prescribe one universal benchmarking method. Different domains may require peers, historical self, market participants, or task-specific baselines. YF3N0-C should test benchmark sensitivity rather than invent one universal benchmark.

### Note D｜NLP mechanism registry remains open-world

Genesis classes are examples. Future work must not assume replication / compounding / network / feedback / capital / AI / optionality exhaust all nonlinear mechanisms.

### Note E｜Causal arrows are probabilistic, not deterministic

`Persistent Intrinsic Pull → sustained engagement → capability → advantage` is a causal hypothesis chain with mediators and boundary conditions, not a deterministic law. Runtime representation should preserve uncertainty and counter-evidence.

## 9. Pre-YF3N0-C risks to carry forward

### Risk 1｜Retrospective narrative contamination

Historical cases can always be narrated after outcomes are known. YF3N0-C must use prospective or outcome-not-fully-revealed cases with frozen cutoffs.

### Risk 2｜Benchmark arbitrariness

EAA may appear or disappear depending on the reference group. Prospective tests must record benchmark choice and perform sensitivity checks.

### Risk 3｜Domain granularity gaming

A domain can be defined too broadly (“technology”) or too narrowly (“the exact thing I already won at”) to manufacture edge. YF3N0-C needs lawful domain-granularity rules.

### Risk 4｜Leverage-story inflation

Nearly every modern venture can claim AI, network, brand, or scale. NLP requires explicit mechanism, sign, capacity, bottleneck, and falsifier.

### Risk 5｜Obsession survivorship bias

Successful founders are remembered for persistence while failed obsessive founders disappear from narratives. Prospective design must include strong negatives.

### Risk 6｜Force-to-capital leakage

A ForceValueDomain must not become direct investment or concentration advice. Separate capital authority remains mandatory.

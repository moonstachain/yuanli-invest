## 1. Scope Freeze

### 1.1 Domain of validity

YF3N0 is a first-principles causal grammar for:

> **agent-originated, identity-consistent, non-average value creation**

中文：

> **主体内生的、与自身长期身份一致的、非平均价值生成。**

It studies how a subject's persistent endogenous pull can become a task-specific evidenced edge and then enter a real leverage structure capable of generating disproportionate value.

### 1.2 Explicit non-scope

YF3N0 is **not**:

- a universal theory of all success;
- a necessary condition for all wealth;
- a sufficient condition for success;
- a definition of human worth;
- a replacement for SOUL;
- a personality typology;
- a psychometric truth instrument;
- a market-timing model;
- an investment recommendation engine;
- an execution or capital-allocation authority.

Success generated by inheritance, sovereign privilege, natural-resource rents, monopoly grants, structural location, or other exogenous advantages may be economically large without satisfying Three-Non.

### 1.3 Lawful analysis unit

All Three-Non claims must be scoped to:

```text
Actor × ProblemDomain × Context × PointInTime
```

Forbidden form:

> “这个人非对称优势很强。”

Lawful form:

> “At PIT T, actor A shows an evidenced advantage in task/domain D under context C relative to benchmark group B.”

## 4. Primitive 1｜Persistent Intrinsic Pull

Human label: `非理性痴迷`

Canonical label: `Persistent Intrinsic Pull (PIP)`

### 4.1 Definition

A relatively stable tendency for a problem domain or activity to repeatedly and autonomously attract attention and cognitive effort even when continuous external reward is absent.

### 4.2 Causal role

PIP is a:

> **Duration Generator**

It raises the probability of sustained engagement. It does not create truth, talent, or success by itself.

Lawful relation:

```text
PIP → higher probability of sustained engagement
```

Forbidden relation:

```text
PIP ⇒ Advantage
PIP ⇒ Truth
PIP ⇒ Success
```

### 4.3 Constitutional rule

> **Obsess the Problem, not the Answer.**
>
> **痴迷问题，不痴迷答案。**

A person may remain deeply committed to a problem while revising or abandoning specific hypotheses.

### 4.4 Evidence

Candidate evidence may include:

- repeated voluntary return across long time windows;
- sustained attention without direct external reward;
- durable learning behavior;
- repeated investment of time/cognition despite temporary lack of payoff;
- consistent cross-stage recurrence around a deeper problem pattern.

Self-description alone is insufficient.

### 4.5 Falsifiers

PIP should be downgraded when:

- the interest disappears rapidly after external rewards/status are removed;
- the claimed lifelong obsession changes with each market trend;
- no repeated behavior supports the narrative;
- persistence is driven mainly by sunk-cost escalation or inability to disengage from a specific answer.

## 5. Primitive 2｜Evidenced Asymmetric Advantage

Human label: `非对称优势`

Canonical label: `Evidenced Asymmetric Advantage (EAA)`

### 5.1 Definition

A repeated, externally evidenced ability to generate superior task-relevant value relative to an explicit reference group, with meaningful replication friction.

Canonical shorthand:

```text
EAA = Relative Performance Advantage + Replication Friction
```

This is causal grammar, not a calibrated mathematical score.

### 5.2 EAA is task- and benchmark-specific

No context-free advantage claims are lawful.

Required fields conceptually include:

```text
actor
problem_domain
task
context
reference_group
observed_output
replication_friction
known_as_of
```

### 5.3 Evidence ladder

- `E0 SELF_CLAIM` — self-described strength; insufficient.
- `E1 REPEATED_EXTERNAL_PULL` — repeated external demand/feedback; candidate signal.
- `E2 REPEATED_OUTPUT_ADVANTAGE` — repeated superior output on comparable tasks.
- `E3 REPLICATION_FRICTION` — evidence that the edge is difficult to copy because of tacit knowledge, unique combinations, proprietary data, network, reputation, capital structure, time horizon, or comparable barriers.

Only evidence-backed claims may enter EAA candidate status.

### 5.4 Transfer boundary

```text
Past Success != Transferable Advantage
```

An advantage in one domain may not silently migrate to another domain.

### 5.5 Decay

EAA is Point-in-Time state, not permanent identity.

AI, competition, diffusion, regulation, or market-structure changes may shorten its half-life.

Conceptual field:

```text
advantage_half_life
```

No fixed numerical model is authorized yet.

### 5.6 Falsifiers

EAA should be downgraded when:

- a benchmark group easily replicates the output;
- the advantage exists only as narrative/self-report;
- comparable output data do not show meaningful differentiation;
- the relevant task changes such that the old capability no longer matters;
- AI/technology commoditizes the previously scarce capability.

## 6. Primitive 3｜Nonlinear Leverage Potential

Human label: `非线性回报`

Canonical label: `Nonlinear Leverage Potential (NLP)`

### 6.1 Definition

The existence of a real causal mechanism capable of amplifying an evidenced advantage into disproportionate potential value without requiring proportionate additional direct input from the actor.

### 6.2 Why the ontology uses potential/mechanism

`Return` is an outcome. Using realized nonlinear return as an input would create outcome leakage and circular reasoning.

Therefore:

```text
Nonlinear Leverage Potential → possible nonlinear realized return
```

not:

```text
realized nonlinear return → proof that NLP existed
```

### 6.3 Genesis mechanism classes

Illustrative, open-world mechanism classes include:

- replication;
- compounding;
- network effects;
- feedback loops;
- capital leverage;
- IP/software/AI leverage;
- optionality / bounded-left-tail payoff structures.

The registry remains open-world. This document does not claim completeness.

### 6.4 Signed leverage

Leverage can be positive or negative.

```text
Scalable != Positively Nonlinear
```

A scalable loss machine may amplify destruction.

Every NLP candidate must conceptually expose:

```text
mechanism
sign
capacity
saturation
bottleneck
failure_mode
```

### 6.5 Distribution boundary

```text
Heavy Tail != Positive Convexity
```

A heavy-tailed outcome distribution does not by itself prove attractive payoff geometry.

### 6.6 Falsifiers

NLP should be downgraded when:

- scale worsens unit economics;
- incremental output remains tightly proportional to direct input;
- claimed network effects turn negative or saturate early;
- replication cost does not decline;
- the mechanism depends on unverified coordination assumptions;
- capacity ceilings destroy the claimed amplification.

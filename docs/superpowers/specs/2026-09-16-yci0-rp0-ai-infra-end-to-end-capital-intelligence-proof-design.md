## 1. Strategic Thesis

YCI0-RP0 is not another AI thematic research report and not another infrastructure integration project.

It is the first bounded proof that the Yuanli capital-intelligence stack can complete one real loop:

`Reality → Evidence/PIT → Context → Research Compilation → Human Work → Audit → Shadow → Settlement → Learning → Reuse`

The flagship case is AI infrastructure because one case simultaneously exercises macro conditions, hyperscaler capex, semiconductor supply, networking, power/grid constraints, narrative crowding, valuation, cross-asset transmission, and right-tail payoff.

The program succeeds only if one Capital Question is carried end-to-end through machine-owned Reality, human-owned judgment, future settlement, and reusable LearningDelta.

### North-star completion sentence

> **One Question × One Reality Event × One Human Transition × One Learning.**

## 2. Mother Question

The frozen parent question is:

> **Is AI infrastructure Reality still accelerating; is the binding bottleneck migrating from GPU toward networking, power/grid, cooling, or capital efficiency; is market Narrative ahead of Reality; and does current Price still offer right-tail Payoff under survival constraints?**

The first Human Capital Question projected to Notion should be phrased more simply:

> **AI Infra 是否已经进入 Narrative 超前 Reality、但瓶颈与利润池仍在迁移的阶段？**

### Required sub-questions

1. **Reality:** Are hyperscaler capex, data-center deployment, accelerator demand, networking, and power/grid investment still accelerating in Level / Δ / Δ² terms?
2. **Constraint:** Which constraint is binding now, and is the bottleneck migrating?
3. **Narrative:** What does the market collectively believe, how diffused/crowded is that belief, and where does Narrative differ from Reality?
4. **Transmission:** How does the shock propagate from demand to bottleneck to profit pool to asset?
5. **Price:** How much of the thesis is already embedded in price/valuation?
6. **Payoff:** What are Bull / Base / Bear / Hard-Negative outcomes and their asymmetry?
7. **Survival:** If the thesis is wrong, what can be lost and does the error reduce the ability to participate in the next right tail?
8. **Learning:** When Reality settles, which rule changes and becomes reusable context?

**Goal:** prove Brain adds bounded historical/contextual lift without answering the thesis for the user.

**Output:** `AI Infra Reality State Card` with Level / Δ / Δ² for macro and industrial variables.

**Goal:** distinguish what Reality is doing from what the market believes, and map bottleneck/profit-pool migration.

**Output:** `NarrativeTransmissionCard`.

**PASS:** one explicit Narrative Stage, one Narrative Gap hypothesis, at least one transmission chain, at least one hard-negative branch, and explicit LIMITED scientific status.

**Goal:** separate “trend is real” from “asset payoff is attractive.”

**Output:** `PricePayoffCard` with implied belief, Bull/Base/Bear/Hard-Negative, defeat condition, survival cost, and S/C/R/X research mapping.

**PASS:** no deterministic price target requirement; uncertainty and downside are explicit; RESEARCH authority only.

**Goal:** prove machine Reality can safely change Human Work state.

**Output:** Notion Capital Question linked to Research Projection and Audit.

**PASS:** at least one machine event produces either one-stage forward movement or fail-closed regression, with immutable receipt and no human-authored thesis overwrite.

### B7｜Forward Shadow × Settlement

**Goal:** freeze T0 judgment before the answer is known.

**Output:** preregistered Shadow record with T+30 / T+90 / T+180 review points where calendar time permits.

**PASS:** original T0 bundle is immutable; later observations are separate settlement objects.

### B8｜Learning → Brain Reuse

**Goal:** prove the system becomes reusable, not merely retrospective.

**Output:** one `LearningDelta` and one subsequent Brain recall showing the delta can be surfaced for a related future question.

**PASS:** Old Rule / Failure Layer / New Rule / Scope / Evidence / Version are explicit; learning does not rewrite original evidence or T0 judgment.

## 15. State Machine for the Flagship Question

The Human journey is:

`01 DISCOVER → 02 EVIDENCE → 03 NARRATIVE → 04 TRANSMISSION → 05 AUDIT → 06 SHADOW → 07 LEARNING`

### Machine-safe rules

- UNKNOWN/BLOCKED evidence may regress any research stage to `02 EVIDENCE`.
- automatic forward movement is at most one stage;
- Narrative and Transmission remain suggestion/human-assisted while their science is LIMITED;
- `04 TRANSMISSION → 05 AUDIT` may become machine-enforceable when the Native1 binding contract is physically qualified;
- `05 AUDIT → 06 SHADOW` remains denied unless independent shadow authorization exists and the audit qualifies;
- no stage transition grants Capital or Execution authority.

## 22. Four-Week Battle Rhythm

### Week 1｜Freeze & Pipe

- freeze Question Object;
- freeze Wind metric registry;

- Reality State Card;
- Narrative × Transmission Card;

- first Research Projection;

**Gate:** `FIRST_PROJECTION_RESEARCH_ONLY`.

### Week 3｜Human Transition

- machine Reality event into Supabase outbox;
- safe Notion projection;
- one Human state transition or fail-closed regression;
- immutable delivery receipt;
- no overwrite of human-authored thesis.

**Gate:** `ONE_MACHINE_REALITY_TO_HUMAN_TRANSITION`.

### Week 4｜Shadow Genesis

- freeze T0 Shadow record;
- freeze review schedule;
- run synthetic/rehearsal settlement only where future time has not passed;
- freeze LearningDelta schema and reuse test;
- optionally run HF ablation with existing evidence/context.

**Gate:** `FORWARD_SHADOW_PREREGISTERED`.

A real T+30/T+90/T+180 settlement cannot be claimed before the corresponding time exists.

## 25. Success Metrics

RP0 is evaluated on system truthfulness and learning value, not portfolio return.

Primary metrics:

1. **PIT completeness:** proportion of required observations with usable `known_as_of`/vintage lineage.
2. **Traceability:** proportion of state/projection claims linked to evidence refs.
3. **Reality-to-Human latency:** time from admitted material Reality event to Notion work-state projection.
4. **Fail-closed correctness:** UNKNOWN/BLOCKED/authority-denied cases handled without unsafe forward movement.
5. **Context lift:** blind rubric improvement with Brain Context Pack vs no-context baseline.
6. **Audit quality:** hard-negative and defeat-condition completeness.
7. **Learning lift:** later related case retrieves and uses LearningDelta appropriately.
8. **Authority leakage:** must remain zero for Capital/Execution.

Do not use short-horizon investment P&L as the sole or primary qualification metric.

## 26. Stop Conditions

Stop RP0 and require human review if any of these occurs:

1. a design change would grant Capital or Execution authority;
2. a secret/API key must be exposed in GitHub, Notion, logs, or chat-visible artifacts;
3. implementation requires destructive migration of existing evidence/runtime data;
4. Wind/Alice evidence boundaries cannot be preserved;
5. the project expands into multiple independent themes before the flagship question closes;
6. the Forward Shadow T0 record would need post-hoc editing;
7. the existing Native1/Brain interfaces prove incompatible enough that every implementation path becomes a guess.

Operational integration bugs, missing metrics, or provider outages do not by themselves justify architecture expansion; they should fail closed or stay UNKNOWN.

## 27. Anti-Claims

RP0 does **not** prove:

- that AI infrastructure is currently attractive;
- that any named stock should be bought or sold;
- that YMQ predicts markets;
- that Narrative/Herding or Transmission science is fully validated;
- that Wind/Alice is a single source of truth;
- that Yuanli Brain can autonomously decide capital allocation;
- that one flagship case generalizes to every asset;
- that Capital Authority or Execution Authority is authorized.

The proof is narrower:

> **A real capital question can be grounded in machine-traceable Reality, enriched by governed memory, compiled into a falsifiable research projection, moved safely through a Human Workbench, frozen before outcome, settled by later Reality, and converted into reusable learning without authority leakage.**

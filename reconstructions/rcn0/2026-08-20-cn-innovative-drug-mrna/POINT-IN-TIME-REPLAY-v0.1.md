## 6. Competing baselines

RCN0 must beat simpler explanations rather than merely fit the story after the fact.

### Baseline B0 — Broad beta / risk-on

Prediction: innovative drug strength should co-move with broad growth/risk indices.

T0 tension: broad indices were muted and 科创50 was negative.

### Baseline B1 — Earnings/fundamental leadership

Prediction: names/chains with stronger realized earnings/orders should lead the move.

T0 tension: CXO leaders were positive but lagged vaccine/mRNA mapping names; Hengrui fell despite being a core innovative-drug leader.

### Baseline B2 — Low-valuation repair

Prediction: strongest returns should be associated with depressed valuation states.

T0 tension: reported innovative-drug and vaccine valuation percentiles were already high.

### Baseline B3 — Small-cap/high-beta theme effect

Prediction: the move can be explained by size/beta alone.

Status: `not_rejected_at_T0`.

This is an important competing hypothesis because the source notes that smaller vaccine names outperformed larger leaders. Shadow replay must control for market cap, beta, prior momentum and limit-up microstructure before attributing incremental explanatory power to Narrative Distance.

## 7. Explicit falsifiers

The `narrative_led` interpretation should be degraded if replay shows any of the following:

- after controlling for size/beta/momentum, Narrative Distance has no incremental cross-sectional relation to returns;
- independent industrial evidence shows that the strongest vaccine/mRNA mapping names were simultaneously the strongest direct economic beneficiaries;
- capital-flow evidence shows a broad healthcare/growth risk-on shock sufficient to explain the observed cross-section;
- the apparent narrative gradient disappears under a pre-registered universe and classification rather than hand-picked examples;
- source market facts fail independent official verification.

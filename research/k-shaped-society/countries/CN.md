# CN｜1990–2026 K-Shaped Research Dossier

## 1. Scope and data boundary

China is the current-regime target of YKS0, but R0 intentionally distinguishes current structural evidence from historical PIT proof. Official 2025–2026 data provide strong current evidence on productivity/household diffusion and cross-industry profit dispersion. They do **not** yet establish a 1990–2026 vintage-safe K history, firm top-decile profit concentration, or leader-employment diffusion. Historical reconstruction is therefore the central G2 burden.

## 3. D-A Index–Median Divergence

**Desktop prior: INDETERMINATE.** R0 has not reconstructed historical A-share constituents, delistings, corporate actions, cap weights and median/equal-weight outcomes with PIT accounting data. Current industry profit divergence is insufficient to infer an index-versus-median return spread.

## 4. D-B GDP–Household Divergence

**Desktop prior: MODERATE-TO-STRONG CURRENT CANDIDATE.** In 2025 GDP grew 5.0%, per-capita GDP 5.1% and labour productivity 6.1%, while national median disposable income grew 4.4%, urban median income 3.7% and real per-capita consumption 4.4%. The correct inference is weaker diffusion of incremental productivity to the median household, not a claim that household income failed to grow.

## 5. D-C Profit–Employment Divergence

**Desktop prior: STRONG RESEARCH QUESTION, NOT YET PROVEN.** Official 2025–2026 industrial data show extreme cross-industry profit dispersion. However, R0 does not yet possess the employment-share and firm concentration evidence needed to prove that the profit winners also created disproportionately fewer jobs.

## 6. Candidate regime episodes

- 1990s–2000s: broad industrialisation/diffusion may be closer to `KR0 BROAD_DIFFUSION`; this remains a replay question rather than a retrospective label.
- 2010s–mid-2020s: candidate transition toward `KR1 PRODUCTIVITY_DIVERGENCE` / `KR2 CAPITAL_CONCENTRATION` as digital/high-end manufacturing control points gain importance.
- 2024–2026: current evidence supports a `REGIME_TRANSITION` candidate, not a settled KR2/KR3 assignment.

## 7. Asset-transmission evidence

The allowed current hypothesis is that sector/firm profit capture can shift relative earnings power toward new control points. It must still pass through ownership, valuation, discount rates, liquidity and market expectations before any relative-return claim. R0 therefore does not infer “new economy profit growth -> index buy.”

## 8. Counterevidence and hard negatives

- `K ≠ Gini`: WDI China Gini fell from 37.1 in 2020 to 35.7 in 2021 and was 36.0 in 2022; a structural K process need not map one-for-one into a rising income Gini.
- `HN1 CYCLICAL_DISPERSION`: temporary sector profit cycles can mimic K01/K03 widening.
- `HN4 DATA_ARTIFACT`: classification changes, revised macro history and changing listed-firm universes can create hindsight divergence.
- Policy redistribution/predistribution may compress downstream K even if production K widens.

## 9. PIT readiness by period

- 2025–2026 NBS releases: exact dated current evidence, `CURRENT_ONLY` for R0's historical replay purpose.
- 1990s–2010s macro vintage history: `PIT_AUDIT_REQUIRED`; China-specific archival coverage is a major G2 risk.
- Firm-level profit/employment and A-share historical constituent data: `UNKNOWN/PIT_AUDIT_REQUIRED`.
- WDI Gini: retrospective `CURRENT_ONLY`; original country survey vintage chain not established here.

## 10. Comparability limits

China's industrial enterprise definitions, listed-company universe, household survey definitions and urban/rural income structure require country-native treatment unless a crosswalk is documented. Industry-level profit growth cannot be directly compared with firm-level top-decile profit shares in the US/Japan/Korea.

## 11. Current scientific status

`STRONG_PRIOR / FORMAL_PIT_VALIDATION_REQUIRED` for the importance of distribution/diffusion as a macro lens. D-B has current official support; D-C remains a high-priority hypothesis; D-A is currently indeterminate. No full K regime is settled.

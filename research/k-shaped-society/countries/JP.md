# JP｜1990–2026 K-Shaped Research Dossier

## 1. Scope and data boundary

Japan is one of R0's strongest **GDP/productivity-to-household diffusion** cases and an important hard negative for simplistic asset mapping. BOJ research provides strong retrospective evidence on the productivity–real-wage gap and large-versus-small firm profitability; JPX provides a recent falsifier in which Large70 underperformed broad TOPIX. Exact historical T0 bundles remain unconstructed.

## 3. D-A Index–Median Divergence

**Desktop prior: MIXED / FALSIFIER AVAILABLE.** As of 31 Oct 2025, TOPIX Large70 underperformed TOPIX over one and three years. That is a direct warning against converting firm K into a rule that large-cap equities must outperform. Valuation, sector mix, discount rates and cycle remain separate Price Gate variables.

## 4. D-B GDP–Household Divergence

**Desktop prior: STRONG GOLD CANDIDATE.** BOJ research describes a long period from the late 1990s in which productivity increased while real wages were broadly flat. Wage rigidity and prioritisation of employment stability are plausible mechanisms for weak diffusion from productivity to typical worker income.

## 5. D-C Profit–Employment Divergence

**Desktop prior: STRONG CANDIDATE.** BOJ reports small-firm operating profit per employee at 40% of large firms in 1990 but only 13% in 2024. This establishes a large firm-size profitability gap, but formal D-C still needs PIT employment/share evidence rather than inferring jobs from profit-per-worker alone.

## 6. Candidate regime episodes

- Late 1990s–2010s: `KR1 PRODUCTIVITY_DIVERGENCE` / labor diffusion failure candidate.
- Later period: possible `KR2 CAPITAL_CONCENTRATION` in firm outcomes, but K06 wealth evidence is missing.
- 1990s asset collapse remains an `HN2 AGGREGATE_STAGNATION` control: weak households during broad stagnation must not be mislabeled as K solely because outcomes are poor.

## 7. Asset-transmission evidence

Japan demonstrates why K is not an asset timing engine. A firm/productivity K state can coexist with broad-market or large-cap return patterns that do not conform to a simple leader-wins rule. Asset inference must pass through earnings capture, valuation, rates and liquidity.

## 8. Counterevidence and hard negatives

- `HN2 AGGREGATE_STAGNATION`: post-bubble weakness can produce poor household outcomes without a K-specific mechanism.
- `HN6 ASSET_LEVEL_DROP`: asset prices can fall even while structural firm dispersion exists.
- 2025 Large70 underperformance is a direct H7 falsifier candidate.

## 9. PIT readiness by period

- BOJ 2025/2026 analyses: `CURRENT_ONLY` retrospective research.
- OECD macro revisions: `PIT_RECONSTRUCTABLE` candidate for selected short-term series.
- Historical firm-size profit series: current retrospective statements exist; exact publication vintages are `UNKNOWN/PIT_AUDIT_REQUIRED`.
- JPX 2025 factsheet: exact dated current evidence, but not a multi-decade replay bundle.

## 10. Comparability limits

Japan's deflationary wage-setting regime and employment-stability norms make labor pass-through institutionally distinct from the US. Profit-per-employee is not identical to productivity and cannot be used as a direct cross-country level metric without a crosswalk.

## 11. Current scientific status

`STRONG_PRIOR / FORMAL_PIT_VALIDATION_REQUIRED` for D-B; D-C is strong candidate; D-A remains mixed and provides a useful falsifier. H7 must remain conditional.

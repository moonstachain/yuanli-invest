# KR｜1990–2026 K-Shaped Research Dossier

## 1. Scope and data boundary

Korea is R0's strongest **Winner-Less-Hiring** desktop candidate and also one of its best rediffusion controls. OECD evidence simultaneously shows a deep large-firm/SME productivity gap and a pronounced 2013–2024 SME catch-up. This makes Korea useful precisely because it can falsify a permanent one-way K story. Formal historical replay remains unbuilt.

## 3. D-A Index–Median Divergence

**Desktop prior: STRONG CANDIDATE, HIGH CYCLICAL CONFOUNDING.** STOXX South Korea 200 outperformed the Total Market in 2025 and in the displayed July-2026 YTD/one-year windows. Semiconductor and sector beta, valuation and cycle can independently generate the same pattern, so this is not yet K-specific evidence.

## 4. D-B GDP–Household Divergence

**Desktop prior: MODERATE.** The structure supports a plausible gap between advanced export champions and typical household/labor outcomes, but R0 lacks a sufficiently symmetric median-income/wage/consumption PIT bundle to classify this target strongly.

## 5. D-C Profit–Employment Divergence

**Desktop prior: VERY STRONG GOLD CANDIDATE.** OECD reports weakened trickle-down as large business groups internationalised and became more capital/technology intensive; the top 30 groups represented only 2.7% of employment in 2017. At the same time SMEs employ more than four-fifths of workers and operate at much lower productivity.

## 6. Candidate regime episodes

- 1990s–2010s: possible progression from broad industrial catch-up to `KR1 PRODUCTIVITY_DIVERGENCE` / `KR2 CAPITAL_CONCENTRATION`.
- 2013–2024: explicit **counter-signal** as SME productivity grows much faster than large-firm productivity; candidate `KR5 REDIFFUSION` at the margin rather than a full level reversal.

## 7. Asset-transmission evidence

Large export/technology champions can capture disproportionate earnings while employing a small share of workers, creating a plausible K-to-relative-equity-transmission path. But South Korea 200 outperformance can also be semiconductor-cycle beta; asset conclusions must remain conditional on earnings breadth, valuation, rates and liquidity.

## 8. Counterevidence and hard negatives

- `HN8 REGIONAL_CATCHUP / REDIFFUSION`: OECD's 2013–2024 SME productivity catch-up directly contradicts a monotonic firm-K story.
- `HN7 SECTOR_ROTATION`: semiconductor concentration can mimic D-A.
- High SME employment means employment diffusion and productivity diffusion can move in opposite directions.

## 9. PIT readiness by period

- OECD 2018/2023/2026 structural studies: `CURRENT_ONLY` retrospective evidence in R0.
- Selected OECD short-term macro series: `PIT_RECONSTRUCTABLE` candidate, exact T0 coverage pending.
- STOXX recent comparison: `CURRENT_ONLY`.
- Firm-level historic profit/employment shares: `UNKNOWN/PIT_AUDIT_REQUIRED`.

## 10. Comparability limits

OECD SME definitions and productivity measures differ by country and in the cited 2026 analysis are based on turnover per person employed, not a universal TFP concept. Korean business-group evidence cannot be mechanically mapped to listed-index constituents.

## 11. Current scientific status

`STRONG_PRIOR / FORMAL_PIT_VALIDATION_REQUIRED` for H2/D-C. Korea is simultaneously a strong K case and a required KR5/HN8 counterexample because recent SME catch-up is material.

# YKS0-R0｜Evidence Authority × PIT Readiness Policy

**Authority:** `RESEARCH_CANDIDATE_ONLY`  
**Scientific ceiling:** `STRONG_PRIOR / FORMAL_PIT_VALIDATION_REQUIRED`

## 1. Three-layer separation

Every decisive statement preserves three non-interchangeable layers: **Source Fact** (what the source actually states), **Research Inference** (Yuanli's interpretation), and **Scientific Status** (desktop prior, preregistered hypothesis, replay-qualified result, or falsified claim). An authoritative source does not by itself create a scientific settlement.

## 2. Evidence grades

| Local | Meaning | Repository projection |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | national statistical office / central bank / ministry / official administrative data | A |
| A2 | OECD / IMF / World Bank / BIS / Eurostat / UN / ILO | A |
| A3 | peer-reviewed or high-quality academic evidence | B |
| B1 | index provider / industry primary / audited issuer disclosure | B |
| B2 | transparent working paper / research institution | C |
| B3 | reputable secondary synthesis with reproducible references | C |
| C1 | practitioner interpretation with evidence links | C |
| C2 | narrative / press / interview input for hypothesis generation | D |
| D | unverified, ambiguous, inaccessible, or definition-incompatible | D |

Projection is one-way and non-upgrading.

## 3. PIT readiness

Every source and candidate case declares exactly one status: `PIT_NATIVE`, `PIT_RECONSTRUCTABLE`, `CURRENT_ONLY`, `BACKTEST_OR_RESTATED`, or `UNKNOWN`.

`PIT_NATIVE` means the source physically preserves the relevant historical release/vintage. `PIT_RECONSTRUCTABLE` means exact T0 reconstruction appears possible but R0 has not yet proven the bundle. `CURRENT_ONLY` is valid for current or retrospective research but not historical T0 visibility. `BACKTEST_OR_RESTATED` identifies later reconstruction/backtest/revised history. `UNKNOWN` fails closed.

Formal replay admission requires `PIT_NATIVE`, or a separately validated `PIT_RECONSTRUCTABLE` source promoted by G2. R0 does not perform that promotion.

## 4. Two-clock and revision law

`published_at`, `known_as_of`, and `observed_at` are different facts. Later physical retrieval never grants information to an earlier T0. If exact `known_as_of` is not established, keep it `null` and prohibit formal replay use. A revision creates a new vintage; it never overwrites the earlier vintage.

## 5. Exact locator and numeric claims

Every numerical claim requires a source URL plus a logical locator such as a table, figure, series, section, paragraph, or dated factsheet. A generic landing page is insufficient for decisive numerical evidence.

## 6. Counterevidence

Support accumulation cannot erase contradictory evidence. Every country dossier, K-dimension cell, hypothesis, and integrated report must preserve material counterevidence and candidate hard negatives.

## 7. Cross-country comparability

Direct five-country level comparison is permitted only under a common definition or documented crosswalk. Otherwise evidence is `COUNTRY_NATIVE_ONLY` or `NOT_COMPARABLE`. Similar labels do not create comparability.

## 8. Asset authority boundary

R0 may record only the research path `KRegime -> Value Capture -> Earnings/Cash-flow Distribution -> Ownership -> Price/Valuation/Discount Rate/Liquidity -> Relative Return Candidate`. R0 creates no target price, buy/sell call, recommended weight, target weight, position size, broker action, paper order, live order, or execution authority.

## 9. Scientific ceiling

R0 may conclude `STRONG_PRIOR / FORMAL_PIT_VALIDATION_REQUIRED`. It may not issue an unconditional scientific PASS. G2 owns vintage-safe Reality reconstruction; G3 owns preregistered blind replay.
